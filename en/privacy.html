Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

What data I collect

Through the form on kotovich.uk: your name, a contact (Telegram or phone) and a description of your task. Only what you type in yourself.

Why

To reply to your request and discuss the project. The data isn’t used for other purposes, such as mailings, without separate consent.

Where it is stored

The request is sent to me in Telegram. The site keeps no separate database of requests. The site runs on Cloudflare infrastructure, which may process technical request data (such as an IP address) for security and operation.

Sharing with third parties

I don’t sell or share your data with third parties, other than the services the form needs to work (Telegram, Cloudflare).

Your rights

You can ask me to delete your request or tell you what data I hold. Write to ai@kotovich.uk or on Telegram @yura_kotovich.
